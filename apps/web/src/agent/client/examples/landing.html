Questions.

Which calendars work?

Any calendar that speaks the standard calendar format. Quietly reads free and busy times; event titles stay private.

Will it move my meetings?

Never on its own. It suggests a new time and you decide with one tap.

Can I pause it?

Yes. One switch turns protection off for a day, a week, or until you turn it back on.
